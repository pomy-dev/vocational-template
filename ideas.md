# Loommark Portal — Design Direction

## Three directions considered

### Theme Name: Loommark Editorial
Very Brief Intro: A warm, tactile operations portal inspired by woven textiles, printmaking, and archival school records. It pairs parchment, indigo, and terracotta with a calm editorial rhythm.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Kiln & Ledger
Very Brief Intro: A material-first interface built around clay, charcoal, and workshop labels, with compact utility layouts and a grounded industrial character.
Probability: 0.04

### Theme Name: Indigo Signal
Very Brief Intro: A crisp, high-contrast dashboard with indigo surfaces and saffron signals, designed for fast scanning across dense student records.
Probability: 0.02

## Selected approach: Loommark Editorial

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial brutalism softened by hand-printed craft references: confident typography, visible structure, paper-like surfaces, and deliberate asymmetry.

### Core Principles
1. **Records should feel cared for.** Data tables use generous row height, strong hierarchy, and human-readable labels.
2. **Material creates trust.** Parchment surfaces, ink lines, woven motifs, and tactile shadows make the product feel like a living school archive.
3. **One focal gesture per screen.** Each screen has a clear primary action instead of a wall of equal-weight controls.
4. **Craft over decoration.** Texture and color support navigation and school identity rather than becoming ornamental noise.

### Color Philosophy
Warm parchment is the base because it lowers visual fatigue and evokes paper registers. Deep ink navy anchors text and structure. Terracotta is the signature action color, referencing fired clay and dyed thread. Saffron is reserved for attention and pending states; sage marks completed work.

### Layout Paradigm
A persistent left rail creates an institutional workspace, while content pages use offset editorial columns rather than centered card grids. Hero bands, ledger tables, and side notes create a sense of a well-kept archive.

### Signature Elements
- A small woven-shuttle emblem used in the rail, login screen, and school identity blocks.
- Thin ink rules and micro-labels that mimic cataloguing marks.
- Offset terracotta tabs and corner notches for primary actions and status.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like turning a page: clear focus, immediate feedback, and subtle movement. Modals are calm and spacious; filters stay visible; actions are named in plain language.

### Animation
Use 180–240ms ease-out transitions. Cards rise by 2px on hover, active buttons press to 0.98 scale, and page sections fade upward in a staggered 40ms rhythm. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
Use Fraunces for display headings and IBM Plex Sans for interface copy. Fraunces creates a crafted editorial voice; IBM Plex Sans keeps dense records exceptionally readable. Headings are compact and confident; micro-labels are uppercase with tracking.

### Brand Essence
Loommark is a school operations portal for institutions that turn making into a livelihood, helping secretaries keep student records as thoughtfully as the work students produce. Personality: **grounded, generous, exacting**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are plainspoken but evocative. CTAs are direct, useful, and never salesy.
- “Keep the register close.”
- “Add the next maker.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The mark is an abstract woven loom shuttle intersecting a small sunburst, rendered as a simple solid emblem. The wordmark is set in a distinctive high-contrast serif treatment rather than a default sans-serif lockup.

### Signature Brand Color
**Terracotta thread — #C9664A**
